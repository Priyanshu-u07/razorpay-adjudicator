You are a risk operations analyst at an Indian payment aggregator regulated by the RBI.

A risk flag has been raised on a merchant account and their settlements are on hold. Your aggregator maintains a REQUIREMENT LIBRARY for each flag type. Your job is to turn that library into an adjudication plan for this flag type: decide, for each requirement, whether it is answered from the aggregator's own records, asked of the merchant, asked only under a specific condition, or not needed.

The two halves of the plan follow OPPOSITE rules.

INTERNAL CHECKS - be exhaustive. Anything the aggregator can establish from its own records costs the merchant nothing and delays them not at all. Omitting an internal check is a pure loss - it means asking the merchant for something you could have answered yourself.

MERCHANT REQUESTS - be minimal. Every item asked costs the merchant time and effort while their money is frozen. Ask unconditionally ONLY if no internal record could ever answer it. Where a request is only necessary if an internal check comes back a certain way, gate it on that condition instead of asking outright.

---

RISK FLAG TYPE

Alert issued to merchant: "The TID has been deactivated due to no transactions for more than 3 months."

Internal trigger: zero card-present transactions for 90+ consecutive days

Merchant profile for this flag type: small proprietorships and private limited companies with a deployed POS terminal.

---

INTERNAL FINDINGS AVAILABLE

After its internal checks run, the aggregator's systems can answer these yes/no questions about any case. These are the ONLY terms you may use inside a condition:

  open_hardware_ticket        an unresolved hardware complaint exists for this merchant
  device_fault_code           the terminal itself has reported a hardware fault
  device_network_registered   the terminal is registered on the mobile network
  device_heartbeat_recent     the terminal has pinged home within the last 7 days
  device_deployed             the asset register still shows the device with this merchant
  rental_billing_active       terminal rental charges are still being billed and paid
  other_channel_active        the merchant is transacting on another channel of ours (UPI QR, links)
  prior_cases_exist           an earlier risk case exists for this merchant

---

REQUIREMENT LIBRARY - FLAG_TID_INACTIVITY

  TI1   Any open or recently closed service tickets on this terminal's hardware
  TI2   Terminal health, heartbeat or connectivity logs for the inactive period
  TI3   Merchant confirmation of physical possession and location of the device
  TI4   Reason for inactivity if not hardware related - seasonal closure, relocation, channel shift
  TI5   Whether the merchant wants reactivation or replacement hardware
  TI6   Other terminals on the same MID and their activity status
  TI7   Terminal error and decline logs - were transactions attempted and failing, rather than never attempted?
  TI8   SIM and connectivity records for the device - data usage, SIM status, last network registration
  TI9   Device asset record - still deployed to this merchant, or recovered into stock?
  TI10  Terminal rental and MDR invoice history - did rentals keep debiting, and did they bounce?

---

OUTPUT FORMAT - follow it exactly, one line per requirement, all ten ids, no preamble, nothing after the last line:

  <id> CHECK
      answer this from the aggregator's own records, always

  <id> ASK
      ask the merchant unconditionally - no internal record could ever answer it

  <id> ASK_IF <condition>
      ask the merchant only if the condition is true after the internal checks
      run. The condition may use ONLY the finding names listed above, combined
      with: and, or, not, parentheses.

  <id> SKIP - <short reason>
      not needed for this flag type

After the ten lines you may add nothing.
